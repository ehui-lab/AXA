# AXA Global Healthcare — Outpatient Claim Submission Guide

## Overview
This guide covers how to submit outpatient medical claims for dependants on the AXA Global Healthcare Customer Online portal.

- **Portal:** https://customer.axaglobalhealthcare.com
- **Policy:** see `reference/secrets.json`
- **Account email:** see `reference/secrets.json`

---

## Step 1: Log In
1. Navigate to https://customer.axaglobalhealthcare.com
2. Enter email and password (pre-saved in browser)
3. If prompted with "An active session already exists" -> scroll down and click **Continue**
4. Click **Log in** and wait for the dashboard to load

---

## Step 2: Navigate to Claim Submission
1. From the home dashboard, scroll down to find the **"Make a claim"** panel
2. Click **MAKE A CLAIM**
3. On the "How to make a claim" page, scroll down to the **"Out-patient treatment"** section
4. Click **Submit invoice**

---

## Step 3: Acknowledge the Notice
A "Please note" pop-up will appear. Read it, tick **"I acknowledge"**, then close the dialog (it closes automatically or click X).

---

## Step 4: Fill in the Claim Form

Work through each field in order:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Who is this invoice for? | Select the relevant family member (e.g. Master Harvey / Master Harris) |
| Is the patient over 16 years old? | No (for children) |
| Do you have a claim number? | No |
| Could this be caused by an accident? | No |
| Describe the symptoms | Copy from receipt diagnosis (e.g. "Upper Respiratory Infection Acute" / "Bronchitis") |
| When were you first aware of symptoms? | Date on the receipt (e.g. 16/05/2026) |
| What treatments are needed? | **Consultation and medication** |
| When did the treatment take place? | Date on the receipt |
| Where is the treatment taking place? | **Hong Kong** |
| Have you already paid the invoices? | **Yes** |
| How much are you claiming? | Total amount from receipt (e.g. 824 or 885) |
| Currency of the invoice | **Hong Kong Dollar** |
| Is this your preferred payment method? | **Yes** (HSBC account pre-saved) |
| Upload supporting documents | Upload photo of the receipt |
| Are the invoices/documents in English? | **Yes** |

5. Click **Save & review form**

---

## Step 5: Review & Submit
**IMPORTANT — Claude STOPS here. Eric clicks Submit.**

1. Check the summary page carefully against the receipt
2. Tick **"I confirm the information provided is accurate and complete"**
3. Tick **"I have read and understood the Privacy Policy"**
4. Click **Submit form**
5. Note the **reference number** shown on the confirmation page

---

## Step 6: Repeat for Each Family Member
Each person requires a **separate claim submission**. Repeat Steps 2-5 for each receipt.

---

## Tips & Notes
- **One claim per person per visit** - do not combine multiple people into one claim
- **Keep original receipts** - AXA may request them if they have queries
- **Review time:** Up to 48 hours
- **Reimbursement:** Paid directly to the HSBC bank account on file
